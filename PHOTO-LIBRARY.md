# Photo library, Santa'lana Builders

All 37 images below came out of the **Santa'lana Builders capability statement PDF**
(`Copy of Company Profile booklet SLB.pdf`), which turned out to be the best source
available: the photos embedded in it are the original professional shots at 900 to
4000px, well beyond anything reachable from the Instagram grid.

Every file has been renamed to a descriptive slug, capped at a 2000px long edge and
re-encoded as progressive JPEG at quality 84. The library went from 13.9MB to 5.1MB.

Project attribution comes from the PDF page each photo sat on, so the suburb
assignments are reliable. The descriptions are written from looking at each image. They
are what sits in the `alt` text and the gallery captions on the site, and they work as a
starting point for Instagram captions or listing copy.
